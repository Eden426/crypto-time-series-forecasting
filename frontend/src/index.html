How should I read this forecast?
The model looks at 60 days of open, high, low, and close prices to estimate the next daily close. The chart shows up to 30 recent closing prices.
The confidence indicator is calculated from recent volatility. It is not a measure of prediction accuracy. Market conditions can change quickly, and forecasts can be wrong.
